Meet Alex J. Heyen

With extensive experience spanning multiple facets of the real estate and financial sectors, I have dedicated my career to providing comprehensive, seamless solutions for my clients. My approach is built on integrity, deep market knowledge, and an unwavering commitment to exceptional service.

As a dual licensed professional, I bridge the gap between complex mortgage structures and dynamic real estate markets. Whether you are navigating property acquisition through Flatwater Realty, securing optimal financing via District Lending, or protecting your investment with Flatwater Title and Escrow, you receive holistic guidance tailored to your unique goals.
